Behind SoFlo Stays

Discovering South Florida's Best Escapes

Curating authentic coastal accommodations that deliver comfort, service, and true tropical character.

Who We Are

We’re a small group of local travel writers and hospitality lovers based in South Florida. As the sister site to SoFlo Bites, our neighborhood restaurant guide, we’re helping locals and visitors discover the best places to eat.

Now, we’re doing the same for where to stay — creating a curated hotel directory that highlights properties with real character, genuine hospitality, and true South Florida charm.

Why We Created SoFlo Stays

Most booking platforms are crowded with sponsored listings, generic descriptions, and reviews that don’t always reflect the real guest experience. We wanted to build a guide that cuts through the noise and focuses on hotels that actually deliver a memorable stay — from Art Deco gems in Miami Beach to peaceful coastal retreats in Naples and Key West.

Every hotel we feature is hand‑selected based on its atmosphere, location, amenities, and how well it captures the spirit of South Florida. We also highlight bookable experiences and local dining nearby, so you can plan your stay and your adventures in one place.

Our Selection Criteria

We choose hotels based on three core pillars:

  • True Local Vibes: properties with tropical foliage, historic architecture, local art, or unique coastal views.
  • High‑Value Perks: rooftop bars, private beaches, complimentary bikes, spa access, and other standout amenities.
  • Local Food Connections: nearby dining recommendations from SoFlo Bites, focusing on authentic neighborhood favorites over tourist traps.

Have a hotel suggestion? Submit it through our Suggest a Hotel form.

Our Mission

To connect travelers with local, memorable, and high‑quality accommodations across South Florida — supporting the region’s hospitality, dining, and tourism communities.
